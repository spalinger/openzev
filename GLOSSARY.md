# OpenZEV tariffs

These terms distinguish a tariff's dated configurations from the recurring price bands within them.

## Language

**Tariff series (Tarif)**:
The same charge followed over time through its successive tariff versions. Renaming a tariff changes the name for all its versions.

**Tariff version (Tarifversion)**:
A tariff's configuration for one validity window. A static energy version contains its own price bands; fixed-fee and dynamic versions use their respective pricing rules.

**Validity window (Zeitraum)**:
The calendar dates during which a tariff version applies, with an optional end date.
_Avoid_: Periode, period (ambiguous with a recurring price band).

**Price band (Preisband)**:
A price or percentage with a recurring schedule, such as HT/NT hours, weekdays or seasons, belonging to one tariff version.
_Avoid_: Tarifperiode, tariff period (ambiguous with a version's validity window).
